Your data stays on your phone.

Last updated November 25, 2026

Stashlist is built to keep everything on your phone. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your data.

What Stashlist does not do

  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or behavioral tracking of any kind.
  • It does not have user accounts, logins, or cloud sync.
  • It does not sell your data or share it with any third party, except as described below.

What stays on your phone

Everything you save in Stashlist — titles, posters, genres, status, priority, notes, your own ratings, and any playlists you create — is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

What Stashlist reads and why

Photos: When you choose to import screenshots, Stashlist reads the images you select to find on-screen text, entirely on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition — the image itself never leaves your phone. If you turn on "Delete screenshots after saving" in Settings, Stashlist also requests permission to delete the original screenshots you imported, after you've confirmed the save. iOS always asks you to confirm this deletion itself.

What Stashlist sends over the network

The Movie Database (TMDB): After reading text from a screenshot, Stashlist sends short search queries, not the image itself, to TMDB's API to find the matching movie or TV show title, poster, and synopsis. See TMDB's own privacy policy for how they handle these requests. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Crash reporting (Sentry): If Stashlist crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report — your device model, OS version, app version, and the error itself — is sent to Sentry, the crash-reporting service used to find and fix bugs. This never includes your screenshots, saved titles, notes, or any other content you've saved. See Sentry's privacy policy for how they handle this data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change, and the new policy will be posted at the same location.
